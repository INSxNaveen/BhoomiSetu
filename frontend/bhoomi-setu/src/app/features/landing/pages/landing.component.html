ARCHITECTURE & CAPABILITIES

Built for Enterprise Government Scale

Engineered to eliminate delays, enforce statutory compliance, and guarantee transparency

🗺️

PostGIS Geospatial Alignment

High-resolution cadastral boundary mapping, KML/GeoJSON ingestion, and satellite corridor overlay to pinpoint exact affected parcels.

💳

PFMS Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)

Automated compensation calculation, 100% statutory solatium computation, and direct Aadhaar-linked bank credit with zero intermediaries.

⚖️

RFCTLARR 2013 Compliance

Automated Section 11, Section 19, and Section 21 milestone compliance tracker with proactive SLA violation and bottleneck alerts.

🏡

Second Schedule R&R Governance

Comprehensive tracking of housing plots, one-time subsistence grants, and livelihood rehabilitation packages for displaced families.

🛡️

Role-Based Multi-Agency Access

Strict access boundaries for Central Ministries, State Empowered Committees, District Collectors (CALA), and Project Developers (NHAI/Railways).

📊

National Decision Analytics

Executive MIS dashboards, time-series compensation trends, state performance rankings, and corridor progress heatmaps.
