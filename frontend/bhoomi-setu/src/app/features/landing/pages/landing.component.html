पारदर्शी प्रक्रिया • STATUTORY ACQUISITION WORKFLOW

RFCTLARR Act 2013 Statutory Lifecycle

Step-by-step transparent acquisition process ensuring full landowner rights, social impact assessment, and direct compensation.

चरण 1
📋

Social Impact Assessment (SIA)

Gram Sabha public hearing & preliminary Social Impact Management Plan (SIMP) under Section 4.

चरण 2
📜

Section 11 Notification

Official State Gazette publication with 60-day window for citizen objections and hearing under Section 15.

चरण 3
🛰️

Drone LiDAR Cadastral Survey

Joint measurement survey (JMS) with sub-meter RTK coordinates to establish Bhu-Aadhaar boundary.

चरण 4
⚖️

Section 19 Declaration

Final declaration of acquisition & Rehabilitation and Resettlement (R&R) scheme approval.

चरण 5
💰

Section 23 Award Declaration

LAC passes formal compensation award with 100% Solatium & 12% interest calculation.

चरण 6
💳

PFMS Direct Benefit Transfer

100% direct bank credit to farmer's Aadhaar account & digital mutation in revenue records.
